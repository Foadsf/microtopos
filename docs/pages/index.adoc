:notitle:

Free software · Linux, Windows, macOS

Your manual microscope, now a surface-measuring instrument.

[.lead]
MicroTopos watches the eyepiece camera while you work the knobs as usual. Move the stage and it stitches a mosaic. Turn the fine focus through the sample and it builds a height map, an all-in-focus image and an ISO 25178-style roughness report. No motors, no encoders, no extra hardware.

== What it does

[.center]
Everything is detected from the video alone, so you keep the microscope you already have and the way you already use it.

[.cards]
--
.Stitch while you move
****
image::view-mosaic.png[A stitched mosaic]

Pan the X-Y stage and the field of view joins the mosaic in real time. A blue box shows where the camera is. If tracking is lost, it picks up again by itself when you come back to an area already mapped.
****

.Height maps from a focus sweep
****
image::view-height.png[A height map]

Turn the fine focus slowly from blurry through sharp to blurry again. The sweep is detected, checked and reconstructed in the background, typically in a few seconds.
****

.A metrology report
****
image::view-report.png[The surface report]

Sa, Sq, Sz, Sdr, Sk, Spk, Svk, Sal, Str, grain size (ASTM E112) and more, with ISO 16610 Gaussian filters and every assumption printed next to the numbers.
****
--

== How a session goes

[.center]
A checklist on the left walks you through it, and a one-line coach above the image always says what to do next.

[.flow]
--
.Connect
****
Pick the camera. Exposure is locked and in-camera sharpening is switched off automatically.
****

.Set up
****
Choose the objective, and optionally record dark and flat frames for even illumination.
****

.Calibrate
****
Drag a line over a stage micrometer for µm per pixel; calibrate the focus knob for heights in µm.
****

.Inspect
****
Pan to map, sweep focus to measure. Height maps stay pinned to their place in the mosaic.
****
--

== Built to be honest

[.cards.two]
--
.Every number says where it comes from
****
Uncalibrated lengths are labelled *NOMINAL*. Uncalibrated heights are relative *focus frames*, and so is a sweep whose direction is unknown. A carried-over Z scale is *ESTIMATED*, and simulator results are *SIMULATED*. Pixels without a reliable depth are left empty instead of guessed, and exports carry a validity mask.
****

.Verified against ground truth
****
A built-in self-test (`microtopos-selftest`, 130 checks) runs the whole pipeline on randomised synthetic specimens with known heights. A quick version runs on every release build on every platform. link:development.html#verification[See the measured numbers].
****
